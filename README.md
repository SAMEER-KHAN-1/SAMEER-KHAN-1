## Featured Project

### [Orvyn AI](https://github.com/SAMEER-KHAN-1/Orvyn-AI)

> An Android voice assistant that *operates* your phone instead of just answering questions.

- Sends money through UPI apps by voice
- Orders food through apps like Swiggy and Zomato
- Drives real apps end-to-end, not just to a search result
- Runs **fully offline** as well as online — nothing has to leave the device
